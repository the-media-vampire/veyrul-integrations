# Prepare AI cover artwork for a print-cover handoff

Separate artwork approval from trim size, spine, bleed, safe area, barcode and export requirements.

## Start here

1. Collect the selected printer, trim size, page count and paper choice before sizing the complete cover.
2. Use that printer's current template and verify spine, bleed, text-safe and barcode areas. Keep artwork and layout sources editable.
3. Check the exported PDF and obtain the appropriate proof; track image rights and platform AI-content rules separately.
