# Preserve a character across camera angles

Use the proposed reference-to-keyframe-to-animation route with explicit checks for identity, costume and unseen details.

## Start here

1. Choose an authorized anchor reference and list what must remain consistent across shots.
2. Review angle keyframes before animating. Do not promote a visibly drifted output into the next identity reference.
3. Compare the beginning, middle and end of each shot and the transitions between shots; preserve rejected takes and their reasons.
