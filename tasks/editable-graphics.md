# Turn an AI illustration into an editable SVG

Compare tracing with direct SVG generation and inspect the actual structure before calling the result editable.

## Start here

1. Keep the original raster and decide which shapes, colors and text must remain independently editable.
2. Read the selected tracer or SVG model's options and version. Choose palette and stacking behavior deliberately.
3. Open and render the SVG, inspect paths, groups, text and gradients, and compare the result against the original.
