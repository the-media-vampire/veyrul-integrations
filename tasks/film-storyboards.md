# Turn a script into a reviewable AI shot plan

Compare documented script, character and shot interfaces before commissioning generation.

## Start here

1. Separate scene intent, character continuity, shot composition and generated output in the plan.
2. Read the selected system's documented inputs, model roles and review points; do not assume different projects share a schema.
3. Approve a small shot sequence against story and continuity criteria before expanding production.
