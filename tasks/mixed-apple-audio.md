# Select a usable audio stream from a mixed Apple MOV

Inspect stream metadata before choosing between an ordinary audio track and an APAC stream your decoder may not support.

## Start here

1. Inspect all audio streams, their indices, codecs and channel layouts with the actual installed tool version.
2. Map the intended supported stream explicitly. A filename or first-audio-stream default does not identify the right track.
3. Check duration, synchronization and decoded output. Retain the source when a codec is unsupported.

## Limits

A working ordinary-track fallback does not decode APAC, preserve spatial metadata or prove every Apple recording has a fallback track.
