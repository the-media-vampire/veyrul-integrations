# Align podcast words and speakers with WhisperX

Check language alignment, missing word timings and diarization access before treating a transcript as ready for captions.

## Start here

1. Record the audio language, transcription model and alignment model; retain the original audio and transcript.
2. Inspect missing word times, numbers, currency and overlapping speech. A forced alignment result is not a transcription accuracy guarantee.
3. Treat speaker diarization as a separate stage with its own model access and evaluation; inspect speaker changes before export.
