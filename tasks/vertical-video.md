# Choose between cropping and outpainting a vertical video

Estimate what a 9:16 delivery removes or invents before selecting a reframing method.

## Start here

1. Locate the subject, titles and important actions in the source; choose delivery dimensions and safe placements.
2. Compare a crop or manual reframe against outpainting. Calculate retained and added area, then identify what must remain faithful.
3. Check motion and edge continuity through the complete clip, including titles and captions at the actual delivery size.

## Limits

Area calculations do not measure visual quality. Outpainting invents content and may change details; a centered crop is not automatically a good composition.
